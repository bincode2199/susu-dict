## G0 decision

1. **Plugin engine: QuickJS-NG** in the AppContainer plugin host. It meets every PER01/PER02 number with the smallest footprint. Jint is rejected because its host fails strict AOT analysis and uses twice the cold time and memory. The WebView route is rejected: cold p95 is 4.7 s and 124.5 MiB. The JS slice budget is **250 ms, starting after host JSON materialization**. 100 ms was insufficient for 1 MiB requests ([quickjs-budget-calibration.json](quickjs-budget-calibration.json)). This is a recorded budget change, not a silent one.
2. **Selection: three levels, IA2 kept.** UIA covers 7/10 programs cold; both Electron builds need IA2 cold and Firefox needs priming. Clipboard borrowing stays optional and off by default.
3. **Windows: one WebView2 environment with `TrySuspend` keep-warm and release.** The baseline without suspension fails PER02 warm (186.75 MiB); with suspension the full workload passes.
4. **Budgets unchanged** apart from item 1. PER03 hotkey targets (≤50 ms hot, ≤150 ms cold native feedback, ≤300 ms cold first frame) remain the targets for F03.

5. **Capture: GDI BitBlt** (see the table above); DXGI duplication stays unused.
6. **PER03: accepted as a known gap (product owner, 2026-09-24, [D-66](../../product/RECORD.md)).** The hot (p95 57 ms vs ≤50) and cold interactive (p95 792 ms vs ≤300) results were judged acceptable. Option (a) was taken: the targets stay in TEST-PLAN, the native shell gives feedback at p95 59 ms, and the page follows at about 0.8 s on a cold start. These two misses do not block G0 or later modules and are listed as a known limitation. The options not taken were (b), a longer-lived warm browser (about 90 MiB resident, conflicting with the ≤25 MiB idle budget), and (c), re-measuring before deciding. PER03 is still re-measured on the production shell in F19, on a physical Windows machine ([D-67](../../product/RECORD.md)), and reported against these numbers.

F01 (pure logic) and F02 (config and data) do not depend on these items. To reproduce the interactive measurements (they need a visible, rendering desktop):
