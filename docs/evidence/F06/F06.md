## Scope and contracts

- **Packages:** four built-in packages in `src/Susu.Host/plugins/` (`mymemory`, `tencent-translate`, `deepl`, `openai`). Their error mappings and limits are in the 2026-09-25 work-log entries.
- **Grants:** named signers are checked against the grant use `signer:<scheme>` (PLAN 4.5.2).
- **Streams:** a stream that ends with an error is not a normal end. OpenAI must end with `[DONE]` or a `finish_reason`, or the result is a `network` error and the J02 retry applies. Cancelling through `PluginProvider` always sends `HostSession.Cancel`.
- **UI contract:** `Secret.WriteNew`, `Settings.BindAccount`, `Settings.ReorderService`, `Settings.ValidateProvider`; `ServiceView.credentialTargets`, `plan`, `order`; `offline` on `CardPatch` and `TranslationSnapshot`. Types are generated in `protocol/generated/ui.ts`, and `ContractsGen --check` passes.
- **Plugin CLI:** `susu-plugin` turns bare manifest hosts into https origins (`Origin.TryFromManifestHost`).
- **Not in F06:** selection, OCR and recording entry points; OpenAI `baseUrl`/`model` settings UI (F07); toasts and a "查看日志" action.
