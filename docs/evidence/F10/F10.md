## Scope and contracts

- **F10.1:**
  - **Ports and player:** `Susu.Abstractions/SpeechPorts.cs`; `SpeechPlayer` (`SpeakAsync`, `PlayAsync(acquire)`, `Stop`, `StateChanged`, `Device`, `IdleAsync`). Outcomes: Completed, Stopped, Superseded or Failed (service error, or device NoDevice/DeviceLost/Unsupported/Failed). `SpeechBackend` (`Player`, `Tts`, `DictionaryAudio`, `RequestFor`, `SpeakSelectedAsync`) is reached through `ShellCoordinator.Speech`.
  - **Result files (B07):** a call's response files are released when the call ends unless the caller passes `adoptResultFiles`; the host adopts a result before the call's leases are released.
  - **Native:** `SapiTtsProvider` speaks plain text into a leased 22.05 kHz WAV and deletes partial output on cancel or timeout. `WasapiAudioSink` decodes with Media Foundation and plays through shared-mode WASAPI. SAPI voices are served through the options broker.
  - **P-S01 Microsoft:** `Ocp-Apim-Subscription-Key`; `region` selects the endpoint and grant origin `https://{region}.tts.speech.microsoft.com` (malformed → `eastus`). Escaped SSML with prosody rate; raw MP3 comes back as a handle. 5000 characters; voices from `/cognitiveservices/voices/list`.
  - **P-S02 Google:** `X-Goog-Api-Key`; 5000 bytes. The broker decodes `/audioContent` into a file; errors come from `/error` and are classified by `error.status`.
  - **P-S03 Tencent:** TC3 for the `tts` service. Speed maps to Tencent's −2..6 scale; MP3 from `/Response/Audio`; errors from `/Response/Error`. Chinese and English only: 150 Chinese / 500 other characters. A fixed list of 7 voices.
  - **All three:** 401/403 → auth; 429 → rate_limited with Retry-After; 5xx → network. A success is played only if its bytes are audio. Speed is clamped to 0.5–2.
  - **Dictionary audio:** `DictionaryAudioFetcher` is wired (`FetchClipAsync`).
